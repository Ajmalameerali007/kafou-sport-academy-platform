# Commercial slice

This additive module implements monthly packages, explicit membership acceptance, invoice lines, offline receipts, payment allocation, independent discount/writeoff/credit/refund/freeze controls, entitlement history, renewal reminders, and delivered-session compensation review. All policy defaults remain synthetic; no payment provider or payroll transfer is connected.

## Authorization and API

`public.product_command(action,data,key)` supplies active-account/MFA validation, actor-scoped request-key replay protection, and the existing academy-wide transactional lock. `private.commercial_command` also validates access independently. Every new public table has RLS, authenticated SELECT only, and no client mutation grants. Coach and Sales roles are excluded from commercial reads and commands, even if a finance grant is accidentally attached. Parents read only their own family records, plus active package descriptions. Head Office and Branch need explicit financial grants; Super Admin must meet existing AAL2 requirements.

Scoped financial access also requires an active family link to the exact record branch. Explicitly authorized Head Office retains global finance scope; guardian-own reads remain family-scoped. New and renewed purchases recheck the current active, confirmed branch, sport offering, and package level. Finance calendar checks use Dubai dates.

Separate permissions: `finance.view`, `finance.packages`, `finance.memberships`, `finance.invoices`, `finance.payment`, `finance.discount`, `finance.writeoff`, `finance.credit`, `finance.refund`, `finance.freeze`, `finance.compensation`, `finance.compensation_payment`. Read and write grants remain separate. Commercial audits contain actor, operation, table, and record identifier, without amounts or personal text in the general operational audit feed.

Standalone invoices use `commercial.invoice.create` with a family, exact branch, unique request reference and 1–20 child/package lines. The server copies the current package price and English/Arabic description into immutable ordered line snapshots; authors cannot override price or description. Each child must belong to the invoice family and match the package sport/level, and each package must remain active in that confirmed branch. Quantities are whole numbers from 1–100 and the entire invoice is bounded to 1,000,000,000 AED minor units. Duplicate child/package pairs must use one quantity. A branch-unique reference supplements actor request-key retry protection. Any invalid line rolls back the complete invoice. Existing payment, allocation, receipt, discount and credit commands work against the same invoice ledger. Posting or paying a standalone invoice creates no membership, terms acceptance or session entitlement. Invoice authoring requires `finance.invoices`; its UI also needs `finance.view`. No new permission is seeded by default.

Allocation corrections accept an optional positive `amount_minor` through `commercial.payment.unallocate`. Each correction appends a negative row referencing the original allocation. The aggregate may never exceed the original positive amount. Omitting the amount reverses the remaining allocation only. `finance.refund` remains required; a request retry never repeats the correction. Refundable payment balance continues to subtract all net allocations and recorded refunds.

Cancellation uses a persisted, immutable fifteen-minute preview with an explicit `synthetic-cancellation-v1` policy selection: no credit, unused calendar days after today, or unused entitlements. Calendar proration excludes Dubai's current date; both calculations round credit down to integer minor units. There is no silent default or claim of approved KAFOU commercial policy. Preview and confirmation require current `finance.memberships` and `finance.view`; positive credit additionally requires `finance.refund`. Confirmation must come from the same actor and recomputes the accounting and roster state. Changes, expired previews, unresolved started attendance, existing later memberships, reserved makeup bookings, or ambiguously shared future placements are rejected. These checks include relevant ended/transferred enrollment history.

A confirmed cancellation becomes terminal, ends target active placements (including zero-roster placements), cancels their future ordinary roster rows, releases reservations, preserves consumed units and attendance, and appends invoice credit and any exact allocation reversals required to release overpayment. Existing earned makeup credit remains usable, without another ordinary entitlement grant. The separate offline refund command records an actually completed refund; cancellation never fabricates money movement. Posted credit is not reversible through the generic discount/writeoff correction command. No automatic reinstatement is provided.

Attendance correction requires its separate capability and current child/family access at the exact class branch; revoking the family association denies further branch corrections. An authorized `academy.attendance.correct` may correct a finalized historical roster on a cancelled contract only when that exact roster already has one net consumed entitlement and zero reserved units. It preserves every entitlement entry unchanged and appends immutable, actor-attributed attendance correction evidence. Reserved or consumed makeup credits still block correction; unused credit is revoked or restored according to the corrected absence. This does not enable fresh consumption or recalculate the settled cancellation credit.

Resuming a frozen membership may explicitly request `extend_days`, defaulting to zero. Approved extension days cannot exceed actual elapsed Dubai calendar freeze days or 366, cannot overlap another accepted membership, and never increase session allowance. An immutable `synthetic-elapsed-dubai-v1` amendment retains the old/new expiry, originating freeze episode, actor and reason. The membership expiry is the current projection of that history; a matching amendment in the same transaction is required for an expiry change. No granted permission or approved real academy policy is assumed.

## Accounting and membership invariants

- AED integer minor units, capped at 1,000,000,000 per submitted amount. UI accepts AED decimal amounts and converts only exact two-decimal input. No installments or floating-point accounting in SQL.
- Package prices/terms are versioned by creating a new package; the only package update command changes whether it is offered, enforced by immutable-contract triggers. Families retain access to their accepted package terms even after it stops new sales. An accepted package creates one membership, one invoice, and one snapshot line.
- Monthly validity is `[starts_on, starts_on + one calendar month)`, with month-end clamping from PostgreSQL. Dates cannot overlap for one child, branch, and sport, including pending and frozen memberships. Explicit child sport and optional level match required.
- Unpaid membership remains pending with no entitlement grant. Full invoice coverage or independently approved unexpired credit activates it. Activation creates exactly one grant and reserves every existing future ordinary enrollment within that membership window. If scheduled sessions exceed the allowance, the entire payment/credit activation transaction rolls back, retaining the prior state without a payment, receipt, or partial ledger. The operator must reduce the schedule or select an appropriate package. Credit does not reduce the debt.
- Payment records are offline acknowledgments, with unique branch/method/reference and one durable receipt. Allocation requires matching family and branch and cannot exceed available payment or remaining invoice balance. Partial payment remains pending.
- Discounts and writeoffs reduce only the unpaid balance. Reversals are separate negative entries. Reversal of an allocation or adjustment recalculates membership eligibility; an uncovered previously activated membership becomes suspended. Credit expiry is rechecked before every entitlement use, even without a scheduled status-refresh worker.
- Refunds consume only unallocated, unrefunded payment value. To refund allocated money, reverse the entire chosen allocation first. This explicit step retains accounting history and suspends an uncovered membership. Refund records describe already completed offline refunds and do not move money.
- Freezes block use without changing contractual dates, changing invoice value, releasing class seats, or making refund promises. Resuming rechecks the invoice/credit. Reservation history remains available.
- Renewal uses the prior exclusive expiry as its next start, reuses the accepted package, checks overlap, and has a unique renewed-from link. Repeated renewal returns the same membership. An expired package period must be followed by a new explicit start; it does not backdate renewal. A manually requested branch queue emits once per membership when within seven days of expiry; external delivery stays unconfigured.

## Entitlement integration

Internal-only `private.commercial_roster_event(roster_id,kind,reason)` supports `reserve`, `consume`, `release`, `reverse`. `private.commercial_session_entitlements(session_id,kind,reason)` loops ordinary enrollment rows. Both have no authenticated EXECUTE privilege.

Grant adds available units; reserve moves one available to reserved; consume moves reserved to used; release returns a reservation; reverse returns a used unit. Every transition is a new immutable ledger row. Repeating an already achieved transition is a no-op, while release/rebook and correction/reconsume produce new history. Trial and makeup rows are skipped. A child with no commercial membership in that sport/branch preserves legacy operational behavior. A child who has configured memberships but none covering the session date is rejected instead of silently using legacy access.

Academy commands must authorize the actor before calling these hooks and call them in the same transaction as booking, cancellation, or attendance. Attendance policy must select which statuses consume, and correction must reverse a prior consumption when appropriate. No direct family or staff endpoint grants arbitrary ledger balances.

## Compensation

Rates have explicit nonoverlapping effective date ranges per coach/branch. Accrual requires the academy's separate `class_sessions.delivered_at` and `delivered_by`, an ended noncancelled session, and an applicable rate. Attendance/finalized status alone cannot accrue compensation. One accrual per session, with amount snapshot, followed by a final approval/rejection with reviewer and reason. Approval rechecks delivery. A separate `finance.compensation_payment` capability can record an already completed offline payment, using a unique branch reference and reason. The paid amount derives from append-only settlement entries; a correction appends one negative reversal and restores the approved unpaid balance. Retry keys, the transactional lock, the accrual row lock and exact zero-balance checks prevent double settlement. Every settlement rechecks ended, uncancelled delivery by the actual accrued coach. No command transfers money or executes payroll.

`tests/database/compensation-settlements.sql` passes 18 assertions for unapproved/review-only denial, explicit payment authority, idempotent settlement, duplicate-payment denial, compensating correction, immutable history and Sales/Coach denial. UI choices are filtered by both permissions at the actual accrual branch.
